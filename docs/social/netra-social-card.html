Traffic drops. Netra shows where, keeps the packets and contains it.

Network observability and emergency control on eBPF. No CNI lock-in, no kernel module, no app changes.

A packet is dropped

The drop tracepoint fires and records the reason, the tuple and the kernel function.

DETECT2

Sensors report

Drop reasons, TCP retransmits and resets, listen-queue pressure. Read in about 3 s.

CAPTURE3

Packets are captured

A filtered capture, manual or opt-in auto, through a 16 MB ring buffer into a .pcap.

TRIAGE4

Triage with evidence

Drop Explain, Congestion Map and a blast-radius preview before anything changes.

CONTAIN5

Deny, then let go

A leased deny for 1 min to 24 h. It reverts on its own, and the agent fails open.
